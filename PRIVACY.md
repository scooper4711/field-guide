# Field Guide Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026. This policy covers Field Guide for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

**Field Guide collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
third-party code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else.

## What stays on your device

- **The compendium** ships inside the app and is read offline.
- **PDFs you add** (by choosing them, choosing a folder, or importing from Scrollkeeper) are read on your
  device to show creature and item art and sourcebook lore. Field Guide keeps what it needs from them, and the
  books it was given, in its own storage on your device. Nothing from them is uploaded.
- **Your settings**, such as the game system, category and battle-card view you last chose, are saved on your
  device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## Network

Field Guide makes no network requests of its own. When you tap a link to a web page (for example, Get for a
free web supplement), it opens in your browser, under the browser's and that website's own privacy policies.

On the Mac, Field Guide can ask Scrollkeeper, a separate app, for the list of books you own and to download
one. Those requests go from app to app on your Mac; Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does with them.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send crash reports
for Field Guide to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data
from the app.

## Children

Field Guide collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here, with a new effective date. Questions go to the
